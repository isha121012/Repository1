Cycling.

core definition

Using leg power on pedals to turn a chain to move forward.

Primary Uses

• Transportation: A low-cost, eco-friendly way to commute or carry cargo. • Recreation & Fitness: An aerobic activity that builds lower-body strength (like the quadriceps) and improves heart health. • Sport & Racing: Events regulated globally by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI), including road racing, mountain biking, track cycling, BMX, and major stage races like the Tour de France

Types of Bikes

Road bike, Mountain bike, Fixie
